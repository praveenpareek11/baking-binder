# 13. Jam

**Program:** 13 — Jam
**Loaf size:** Not selectable
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Cooks fruit pulp into jam by heating and stirring

---

## About this program

Makes **jams from fresh fruit and marmalade from oranges**. The machine heats and stirs the mixture.

> ⚠️ **Do not overfill.** If the recipe boils over from the bread pan into the baking chamber, stop the machine immediately, remove the pan, allow to cool, and clean thoroughly.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fruit pulp | 3 cups | Crushed/mushy — process fresh fruit to a coarse pulp first |
| Starch | 0.5 cup | Cornstarch or similar; sets the jam. Some fruits don't need this — see notes |
| Sugar | 1 cup | Adjust up or down to taste |

---

## Method

1. **Prepare pulp:** wash and crush fresh fruit until mushy. You can add a splash of water if the fruit is dry, or leave it out for juicy fruit.
2. Add pulp, starch, and sugar to the bread pan (kneading paddle installed).
3. Lock pan in machine, close lid, start Program 13.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Strawberry / raspberry jam:** these have natural pectin; you can reduce starch to ¼ cup.
- **Orange marmalade:** use 3 cups roughly chopped orange (with some peel for bitterness); reduce starch to 0.25 cup, increase sugar to 1.5 cups.
- **Low-sugar jam:** reduce sugar to 0.5 cup — texture will be looser and shelf life shorter (refrigerate immediately).
- **Add a squeeze of lemon juice** (1 tbsp) for brightness and natural pectin activation.
- **Sterilize jars** before transferring hot jam for storage. Refrigerate after opening.
- **Watch the volume** — start with 3 cups pulp max; more risks overflow.
