# 8. Dessert

**Program:** 8 — Dessert
**Loaf size:** 1000g only (no weight selection in the manual table)
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Sweet baked dessert with rice, milk, and eggs (rice-pudding-like cake)

---

## About this program

This program kneads and bakes a dessert with **more fat and protein than bread** (eggs and milk).

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient (add order) | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Egg | 2 pcs | Whisked |
| **2.** Milk | 1 cup (~237 ml) | Room temperature |
| **3.** Cooked rice (cooled) | 1.5 cups | Not hot |
| **4.** Sugar | 0.5 cup (~100 g) | |
| **5.** Raisin | 0.5 cup (~75 g) | Add at beep cue if available, else with other ingredients |

> Add wet ingredients (whisked egg + milk) first, then rice, sugar, and raisins.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Use cooled cooked rice** — hot rice will scramble the eggs.
- **For chocolate version:** add 2 tbsp cocoa powder + 2 tbsp extra sugar; reduce raisins to ¼ cup.
- **For nutmeg/cinnamon spice:** add ½ tsp of either with the sugar.
- This is more of a baked rice pudding / cake than a traditional dessert — expect a soft, custard-like result.
