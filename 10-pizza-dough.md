# 10. Pizza Dough

**Program:** 10 — Pizza Dough
**Loaf sizes available:** 1000g and 750g
**Crust color adjustable:** No (this program does not bake)
**Type:** Dough preparation (knead + rise, no bake)

---

## About this program

Prepares yeasted dough for **pizza crust, buns, or hand-shaped breads**. The machine kneads and rises the dough — you remove it and bake/shape separately.

## Tweak notes

- **Olive oil instead of neutral oil** gives a more traditional Mediterranean flavor.
- **For Neapolitan-style** (chewier, less oil): reduce oil to 2%, increase hydration to 65%.
- **For focaccia:** double the oil (6 tbsp for 1000g size), proof the removed dough in an oiled pan another 30 min, top with rosemary + flaky salt, bake at 220°C ~20 min.
- **After program ends:** remove dough, divide as needed, shape, top, and bake in a hot oven (230–250°C) for 8–12 min.
- **Store unused dough** in an oiled bag in the fridge for up to 3 days — flavor improves.
