## Important Safeguards

Basic precautions to follow before and during use:

- Check that wall outlet voltage matches the rating plate.
- Not intended for use by persons (including children) with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience, unless supervised.
- Supervise children near the appliance; they must not play with it.
- **Do not touch hot surfaces.** Use handles or oven mitts to handle the hot bread pan or hot bread.
- Do not put your hand inside the oven chamber after removing the bread pan — the heating unit stays hot.
- Never immerse cord, plug, or bread maker in water or other liquid.
- Unplug when not in use and before cleaning. Allow to cool before attaching/removing parts.
- Do not operate with a damaged cord/plug or if dropped, damaged, or malfunctioning. Return for service.
- Use only manufacturer-recommended attachments.
- **Household use only. Do not use outdoors.**
- Do not let cord hang over table edges or touch hot surfaces.
- Do not place on or near a gas/electric burner or in a heated oven.
- Use extreme caution when moving an appliance containing hot contents.
- **Never switch on without a properly placed bread pan with ingredients.**
- Never beat the bread pan on the top or edge to remove it — this damages the pan.
- Always plug into the appliance first, then into the wall. To disconnect: turn off all controls (START/STOP), then unplug.
- Do not place eyes/face close to the tempered-glass viewing window in case the safety glass breaks.
- Appliance has a grounded plug; ensure your outlet is well earthed.
- Do not touch moving or spinning parts during baking.
- Use only for intended purposes.
- Clean the oven interior carefully — do not scratch or damage the heating element tube.
- Extension cord rules: rating ≥ appliance rating; must be 3-wire grounded if appliance is grounded; arrange so it cannot be pulled or tripped over.

**Notice — polarized plug:** One blade is wider than the other. If it doesn't fit, reverse it. Do not modify the plug.

## Control Panel Overview

| Button | Function |
|--------|----------|
| **MENU** | Cycles through programs 1–19. Press repeatedly to select. |
| **WEIGHT** | Selects loaf size: 500g / 750g / 1000g. Adjustable in programs that support weight. |
| **CRUST COLOR** | Light / Medium / Dark. Adjustable only in programs 1–7. |
| **TIMER (+ / −)** | Delays program start in 10-minute increments. Max 15 hours (includes baking time). |
| **START / PAUSE / STOP** | 0.5s press = pause (auto-resumes after 3 minutes if no further input). 3s press = cancel program (beep confirms). |

**Default after power-on:** Display shows `3:00`, Program 1, 750g, Medium crust.

**Delay timer notes:**
- Set delay *after* choosing program, weight, and crust color.
- Do **not** use the delay timer with perishable ingredients (dairy, eggs, milk, cream, cheese).
- The displayed time must include the program's baking time.

## Step-by-Step Operating Instructions

### Before First Use

1. Wash and dry the bread pan and kneading paddle.
2. Check for missing/damaged parts.
3. Set the bread maker on the **Bake** program and run empty for ~10 minutes. Some smoke/odor on first use is normal.
4. Cool, then clean all detached parts again. Dry thoroughly.

> ⚠️ Do not use metal utensils in the bread pan — they damage the non-stick surface.
> ⚠️ The bread maker can wobble during kneading. Place it in the center of the counter, away from the edge.

### Each Use

1. Remove the bread pan by turning **counter-clockwise** and pulling up by the handle.
2. Push the kneading paddle onto the drive shaft inside the bread pan.
3. **Add ingredients in this exact order:**
   1. **FIRST: Liquids** (room temperature)
   2. **SECOND: Dry ingredients** (sugar, salt, shortening, flour)
   3. **LAST: Yeast** — make a small indentation on top of the flour and place yeast there. **Yeast must never touch the salt or liquid before mixing starts.**
4. Place the bread pan firmly back into the chamber — turn **clockwise** to lock. Confirm it's locked.
5. Close the lid.
6. Plug in. A beep sounds; display shows default (Program 1, 750g, Medium).
7. Press **MENU** until the desired program number shows.
8. Press **WEIGHT** for size (500g / 750g / 1000g) — if available for that program.
9. Press **CRUST COLOR** for shade — if available (programs 1–7).
10. Optionally set **DELAY TIMER** with + / − (do NOT use with dairy/eggs).
11. Press **START** once. A beep sounds, `3:00` displays, and the indicator light turns on. Kneading begins (or waits, if delay timer is set).
12. **For add-ins** (fruits, nuts, raisins): the machine beeps 10 times after the first kneading. Open the lid and add them. Available in programs 1–7.
13. When complete: 10 beeps sound and the machine shifts to **Keep Warm for 1 hour**. To end Keep Warm early, hold START/STOP for 3 seconds.
14. Unplug and open the lid using oven mitts.

> 💡 Pre-measure everything including add-ins before starting.
> 💡 Removing bread immediately after baking helps prevent the crust from darkening further.

## Removing the Bread

1. Use oven mitts — the bread pan and chamber are very hot.
2. Lift the bread pan by the handle and turn **counter-clockwise** to unlock.
3. Pull straight up to remove.
4. Turn the pan upside down (handle folded) onto a wire cooling rack and gently shake. Use a non-stick spatula to loosen sides if needed.
5. Let the loaf cool ~20 minutes before slicing. Use an electric/serrated bread knife — not a fruit or kitchen knife — to avoid deforming the loaf.
6. If the kneading paddle stays inside the bread: pry it out with a spatula or small utensil once cool. **Never use your hand** — it can still be hot.
7. Unplug after use.

**Storage:** Sealed plastic bag at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerated for up to 10 days.

## Ingredient Reference

### Flours

| Flour | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Bread flour (high-gluten)** | The most important ingredient. High protein keeps bread from collapsing. Use for most yeast breads. |
| **All-purpose flour** | No baking powder. Suitable for quick breads. Bread flour is better for yeast breads. |
| **Whole-wheat flour** | Heavier texture; smaller loaves. Often combined with bread flour. |
| **Black wheat / Rye flour** | High fiber; must be combined with bread flour for proper rise. |
| **Self-rising flour** | Contains baking powder — use for cakes. **Do not combine with yeast.** |
| **Corn flour & oatmeal flour** | Additive flours for flavor and texture in rough breads. |

### Other Key Ingredients

- **Sugar** — Food for yeast and flavor. White sugar is standard; brown/powdered/cotton may appear in recipes.
- **Yeast** — Living organism; refrigerate. Use bread machine yeast (preferred), active-dry, or instant.
  - 1 tbsp dry yeast = 3 tsp = 15 ml
  - 1 tsp dry yeast = 5 ml
- **Salt** — Improves flavor and crust color, restrains yeast. Don't overuse; bread without salt rises larger but loses flavor.
- **Egg** — Improves texture, nutrition, size. Whisk with other liquids.
- **Grease/butter/oil** — Softens crumb, extends shelf life. Butter should be melted or chopped small before adding.
- **Baking powder** — Used for ultra-fast bread and cake (chemical leavening, no rise time needed).
- **Baking soda** — Often combined with baking powder.
- **Water/liquids** — Always add **first**. Room temperature (20–25°C). Never use dairy with the delay timer.

### Yeast Freshness Test

1. Pour 1 cup (237 ml) warm water (45–50°C) into a measuring cup.
2. Add 1 tsp (5 ml) white sugar; stir. Add 1 tbsp (15 ml) yeast on top.
3. Place in a warm spot for ~10 minutes. **Do not stir.**
4. Fresh yeast will bubble/grow. If it doesn't, the yeast is dead — discard.

## Measuring Correctly

> ⚠️ **Accurate measurement is one of the most important factors** in good bread. Use the included cup and spoon.

**Adding sequence:** Always Liquids → Dry → Yeast (yeast last, on top of flour, never touching liquid or salt).

**Liquid measurement:** Use a clear measuring cup with markings and spout. Place on counter and check level at eye level.

**Dry measurement:** Spoon flour gently into the cup, then level with a knife. **Don't scoop directly from the container** — it can over-pack by up to a tablespoon. Don't tap the cup or pack it down.

> 💡 Stir flour before measuring to aerate it.
> 💡 For small amounts (salt, sugar), use the measuring spoon and level it off.

**For add-ins (fruits, nuts):** Add at the beep after first kneading (programs 1–7) — adding too early diminishes flavor.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

> ⚠️ Unplug and let cool fully before cleaning. Never immerse the housing, cord, or plug.

### Kneading Paddle

If stuck in the bread or pan: add water to the bread pan and soak up to 1 hour. Wipe with a damp cotton cloth.

### Bread Pan

Turn counter-clockwise, lift out. Wipe inside and outside with a damp cloth. **No sharp or abrasive cleaners** — they damage the non-stick coating. Dry completely before reinstalling.

> The bread pan and kneading paddle are **dishwasher-safe**. The pan's exterior or base may discolor with dishwasher use — this is normal and doesn't affect performance.

### Housing and Lid

Wipe with a damp cloth. **No abrasives** — they'll degrade the high-polish surface. **Do not immerse the housing.** Do not disassemble the lid for cleaning.

### Before Storing

Ensure the unit is completely cool, clean, dry, and the lid is closed.

## Warning Displays & Power Interruption

| Display | Meaning | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **HHH** | Internal temperature too high. | Press START/STOP to stop. Unplug, open the lid, let cool 10–20 minutes before restarting. |
| **EE0** | Temperature sensor disconnected. | Press START/STOP to stop. Unplug. Contact an authorized service agent. |

**Power interruption (< 10 minutes):** Process resumes automatically when power returns — no button press needed.

**Power interruption (> 15 minutes):** Unit reverts to default; LCD resets. If the dough has begun rising — **discard ingredients and start over**. If dough hasn't entered the rising phase yet, press START/STOP to resume from the beginning.

## Troubleshooting

| # | Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Odor or burning smell | Spilled flour/ingredients in baking chamber | Cool fully. Wipe excess from chamber with paper towel. |
| 2 | Ingredients not blending; motor straining | Pan/paddle not installed correctly; too many ingredients | Seat paddle all the way on shaft. Measure accurately. |
| 3 | "HHH" shown when starting | Internal temp too high | Cool 15–30 min between programs (unplug, open lid, remove pan). |
| 4 | Window cloudy / condensation | Normal during mixing/rising | Disappears during baking. Clean window between uses. |
| 5 | Kneading paddle stuck in loaf | Common with thicker/darker crust | Remove with spatula once loaf is cool. |
| 6 | Dough not blending; flour built up on sides; loaf coated with flour | Pan/paddle not installed; too many ingredients; gluten-free dough often too wet | Seat properly. Measure accurately, in order. Scrape sides with rubber spatula. Add water 1 tbsp at a time to form a ball. |
| 7 | Bread rises too high / pushes up lid | Too much yeast or flour; paddle missing; forgot salt | Measure accurately. Try decreasing yeast by ¼ tsp (1.2 ml). Verify paddle is installed. |
| 8 | Bread doesn't rise; short loaf | Inaccurate measurement; inactive yeast; lid opened during program | Check yeast/flour expiration. Use room-temperature liquids. Don't lift lid. |
| 9 | Crater in top of loaf | Dough rose too fast; too much yeast/water; wrong program | Don't open lid during baking. Use a darker crust setting. |
| 10 | Crust too light | Lid opened during baking | Don't open lid. Select darker crust. |
| 11 | Crust too dark | Too much sugar | Decrease sugar slightly. Select lighter crust. |
| 12 | Lopsided loaf | Too much yeast/water; paddle pushed dough to one side | Measure accurately. Decrease yeast/water slightly. Whole-grain loaves often shape unevenly — normal. |
| 13 | Loaves come out different shapes | Varies by bread type | Whole-grain/multi-grain is denser and shorter than white. |
| 14 | Hollow or holey bottom | Dough too wet; too much yeast; no salt; water too hot | Measure accurately. Decrease yeast/water. Check salt. Use room-temp water. |
| 15 | Under-baked / sticky dough | Too much liquid; wrong program | Decrease liquid. Verify correct program selected. |
| 16 | Bread mashes when slicing | Bread too hot | Cool on wire rack 15–30 min before slicing. |
| 17 | Heavy, thick texture | Too much/old flour; not enough water | Increase water or decrease flour. Whole-grain is naturally heavier. |
| 18 | Bread pan base darkened/spotted | After dishwasher washing | Normal; doesn't affect the pan. |

**Disposal:** Hand in non-working electrical equipment to an appropriate waste-disposal center per local regulations.
