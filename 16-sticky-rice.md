# 16. Sticky Rice

**Program:** 16 — Sticky Rice
**Loaf size:** Not selectable
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Cooked sticky-rice cake / dessert

---

## About this program

Cooks **polished glutinous (sticky) rice** with water and optional flavorings. The result is a sticky rice cake you can sweeten and flavor to taste.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glutinous rice | 250 g | Soak in water for **about 30 minutes** before use |
| Water | 275 ml | Use with soaked rice |
| Optional add-ins | to taste | Sugar, nuts, sesame seeds |

---

## Method (per manual)

1. **Soak rice:** rinse glutinous rice, then soak in fresh water for ~30 minutes. Drain.
2. **Combine** soaked rice and 275 ml fresh water in the bread pan.
3. **Optionally** add sugar, chopped nuts, or sesame seeds.
4. Lock pan in machine, start Program 16.

> ⚠️ Don't add too much sticky rice — overfilling affects the texture (per manual: "may affect the taste of cake").

---

## Tweak notes

- **For sweet sticky rice (Thai-style):** stir in ½ cup coconut milk + 3 tbsp sugar + pinch of salt after cooking. Top with fresh mango.
- **For Chinese-style sweet sticky rice:** add 2 tbsp sugar, ¼ cup raisins, ¼ cup chopped dates, and 2 tbsp dried longan before starting.
- **For savory sticky rice:** skip sugar; add soy sauce (1 tbsp), shiitake mushrooms (¼ cup, soaked), and Chinese sausage (¼ cup, diced).
- **Make sure the kneading paddle is in place** — it stirs the rice as it cooks.
- **Don't skip the 30-min soak** — unsoaked glutinous rice cooks unevenly.
