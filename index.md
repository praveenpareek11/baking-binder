# Recipes — AMI-BM3-550WDx

All 19 programs from the manual, one file per recipe. Each bread recipe includes:

1. **Quantities table** — 500g / 750g / 1000g loaf sizes side-by-side (as in the manual)
2. **Baker's percentages** — flour = 100%; everything else scales proportionally for any target quantity
3. **Notes** — program-specific tips, what makes the recipe work, and tweak hints

---

## Unit Conventions Used

The manual uses "scoops" and "spoons" — these are ambiguous, so every recipe is also converted to standard units:

| Manual term | Standard | Approx. weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 scoop (large end of measuring spoon) | 1 tablespoon (tbsp) = 15 ml | — |
| 1 spoon (for salt/yeast) | 1 teaspoon (tsp) = 5 ml | — |
| 1 tbsp oil | 15 ml | ~14 g |
| 1 tbsp sugar | 15 ml | ~12 g |
| 1 tsp salt | 5 ml | ~6 g |
| 1 tsp instant yeast | 5 ml | ~3 g |
| 1 cup flour | — | ~140 g (varies by flour) |
| 1 cup water/milk | 237 ml | 237 g |

> ⚠️ The manual is inconsistent in places — for example, "spoons" for salt are clearly teaspoons (2 tbsp salt would be inedible). Conversions below use what produces a sensible loaf.

## Universal Bread-Making Rules (programs 1–7)

1. **Adding order — always:** Liquids → Salt/Sugar/Oil → Flour → **Yeast on top**.
   - Salt and sugar go in opposite corners, never touching the yeast.
   - Yeast goes in a small indentation on top of the dry flour. Never let yeast contact liquid or salt before mixing begins.
2. **Liquid temperature:** Room temperature (20–25°C) unless the recipe says otherwise (e.g. Quick Bread uses 40–50°C).
3. **Yeast is alive** — refrigerate it, check expiration, and use the freshness test if unsure.
4. **Measure flour by spooning** into the cup and leveling. Don't scoop or pack.
5. **Never use the delay timer** with dairy, eggs, cream, or cheese.

## How to Use Baker's Percentages (for tweaking recipes)

Baker's percentages let you scale any recipe to any flour weight. Flour = 100% always.

**Example — Basic Bread baker's %:**
- Flour: 100%
- Water: 64%
- Oil: 8%
- Sugar: 8%
- Salt: 1.2%
- Yeast: 1.5%

**To make a 600g-flour loaf:**
- Flour: 600 g
- Water: 600 × 0.64 = 384 ml
- Oil: 600 × 0.08 = 48 g (~3.4 tbsp)
- etc.

This is how you'd tweak a recipe later — change the flour amount, multiply everything else by its percentage.
