# 1. Basic Bread

**Program:** 1 — Basic Bread
**Loaf sizes available:** 500g, 750g, 1000g
**Crust color adjustable:** Yes (Light / Medium / Dark)

---

## About this program

The standard white/mixed bread program. Uses bread flour (high-gluten) and yeast. This is the default program when you power on the machine.



---

## Ingredients — by loaf size

| Ingredient (add order) | 500g | 750g | 1000g | Manual notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water | 180 ml | 260 ml | 320 ml | Room temperature |
| **2.** Salt | 1 tsp (~6 g) | 1.5 tsp (~9 g) | 2 tsp (~12 g) | Put in one corner |
| **3.** Sugar | 2 tbsp (~24 g) | 2.5 tbsp (~30 g) | 3 tbsp (~36 g) | Put in opposite corner |
| **4.** Oil | 2.5 tbsp (~35 g) | 3 tbsp (~42 g) | 4 tbsp (~56 g) | — |
| **5.** High-gluten (bread) flour | 2.25 cups / 300 g | 2.75 cups / 400 g | 3.5 cups / 500 g | — |
| **6.** Instant yeast | 1 tsp (~3 g) | 1.25 tsp (~3.75 g) | 1.5 tsp (~4.5 g) | On dry flour, no liquid contact |

> **Adding order:** Liquids first → salt & sugar in corners → oil → flour → **yeast last, on top of dry flour (must not touch liquid or salt).**

---

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

Flour is always 100%. Use this to scale to any flour weight.

| Ingredient | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | 100% | base |
| Water | 64–65% | hydration |
| Salt | 1.2–2% | flavor + yeast control |
| Sugar | 7–8% | yeast food + color |
| Oil | 8–11% | softness |
| Instant yeast | 0.9–1.5% | leavening |

**To scale:** pick your flour weight, multiply each percentage to get the other ingredients' amounts.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Crispier crust:** select Dark.
- **Richer flavor:** swap part of the water for milk (room temp; don't use delay timer).
- **Softer crumb:** increase oil to ~12%.
- **Add-ins (raisins, nuts, seeds):** wait for the 10-beep cue after first kneading, then open lid and add — up to ~½ cup per 750g loaf.
- **If loaf collapses:** reduce yeast by ¼ tsp.
- **If loaf is dense:** check yeast freshness; ensure water is room temperature, not cold.
