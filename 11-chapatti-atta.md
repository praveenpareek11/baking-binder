# 11. Chapatti Atta

**Program:** 11 — Chapatti Atta
**Loaf size:** Not selectable (single setting)
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Dough preparation (knead only, no rise, no bake)

---

## About this program

**Knead-only** program for making **chapatti / roti dough**. No rising, no baking. Use the resulting dough on a tawa/skillet to make rotis.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient (add order) | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water | Appropriate amount | Typically ~60% of flour weight — start with less, add more if dry |
| **2.** Salt | 1 tsp | Put in corner |
| **3.** Oil | 3 tbsp | Adds softness |
| **4.** High-gluten flour (or atta) | Appropriate amount | For roti, **whole-wheat atta** is traditional |

> The manual deliberately says "appropriate amount" — chapatti dough is judged by feel, not strict measurement.

---

## Reference quantities (a sensible starting point)

For a batch of ~10–12 rotis:
- **Atta (whole-wheat flour):** 3 cups / ~400 g
- **Water:** ~240 ml (60%) — add gradually
- **Salt:** 1 tsp
- **Oil:** 3 tbsp (~42 g)

## Tweak notes

- **Always use atta (whole-wheat) for authentic roti** — high-gluten white flour will give a tough, chewy result, not soft rotis.
- **The dough should be soft, smooth, and not sticky** — slightly softer than bread dough. Add water 1 tbsp at a time if it looks dry.
- **Rest the dough** (covered) for 15–30 minutes after the program ends — this relaxes the gluten and makes rotis softer.
- **For phulka (puffy roti):** keep the dough slightly softer (62–65% hydration).
- **For paratha:** add 1 tbsp extra oil and 2 tbsp curd to the dough.
