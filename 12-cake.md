# 12. Cake

**Program:** 12 — Cake
**Loaf size:** Not selectable
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Knead + rise (chemical) + bake. Uses baking powder/soda — **no yeast needed for rise**.

---

## About this program

Bakes a simple cake. Rises with **chemical leavening** (self-rising flour + a touch of yeast in the manual, though baking powder alone works). Best to **pre-mix the wet ingredients** before adding to the bread pan.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water | 30 ml | Just enough to help dissolve sugar |
| Egg | 3 pcs | |
| Sugar | 0.5 cup (~100 g) | Dissolve in egg + water using an electric beater |
| Oil | 2 tbsp (~28 g) | |
| Self-rising flour | 2 cups / 280 g | Already contains baking powder |
| Instant yeast | 1 tsp | Optional helper per manual; many recipes skip this for pure cake |

---

## Method (per manual)

1. **Dissolve sugar in egg + water:** combine egg, water, and sugar in a bowl. Mix well with an electric egg-beater until bulky and pale.
2. **Add the other ingredients** (oil, self-rising flour, yeast if using) into the bread pan along with the egg mixture.
3. Place pan in machine, lock, and start Program 12.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Skip the yeast** for a more traditional sponge-cake texture — self-rising flour alone provides enough lift.
- **Vanilla cake:** add 1 tsp vanilla extract with the wet ingredients.
- **Chocolate cake:** replace ¼ cup (35 g) of flour with cocoa powder; add 2 tbsp extra sugar.
- **Lemon cake:** add zest of 1 lemon + 1 tbsp lemon juice (reduce water by 1 tbsp).
- **Don't open the lid during baking** — cakes are very sensitive and will sink.
- **Test doneness** with a toothpick before unloading — bread maker cake programs can be uneven; an extra 5–10 minutes on the **Bake** program (15) may help.
