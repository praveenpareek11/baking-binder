# 7. Gluten-Free Bread

**Program:** 7 — Gluten-Free Bread
**Loaf sizes available:** 500g, 750g, 1000g
**Crust color adjustable:** Yes (Light / Medium / Dark)

---

## About this program

For gluten-free flour blends. The program allows longer liquid uptake. Gluten-free dough is usually wetter than wheat dough and may need scraping during mixing.



---

## Ingredients — by loaf size

| Ingredient (add order) | 500g | 750g | 1000g | Manual notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water | 120 ml | 180 ml | 240 ml | Room temperature |
| **2.** Salt | 0.5 tsp | 1 tsp | 1.5 tsp | Put in corner |
| **3.** Sugar | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | 3.5 tbsp | Put in opposite corner |
| **4.** Oil | 2 tbsp | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | — |
| **5.** Gluten-free flour | 1 cup / 140 g | 1.5 cups / 210 g | 2 cups / 280 g | Pre-mixed GF blend |
| **6.** Corn flour (or oat flour) | 1 cup / 140 g | 1.5 cups / 210 g | 2 cups / 280 g | Replaceable with oat flour |
| **7.** Instant yeast | 1 tsp | 1.25 tsp | 1.5 tsp | On dry flour, no liquid contact |

> **Adding order:** Liquids first → salt & sugar in corners → oil → flour → **yeast last, on top of dry flour (must not touch liquid or salt).**

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

Flour is always 100%. Use this to scale to any flour weight.

| Ingredient | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gluten-free flour | 50% | base |
| Corn flour / oat flour | 50% | structure helper |
| **Total flour** | **100%** | — |
| Water | 43–46% | lower than wheat breads |
| Salt | 0.6–1.5% | flavor |
| Sugar | 11–13% | yeast food |
| Oil | 7–9% | softness |
| Instant yeast | 0.9–1.5% | leavening |

**To scale:** pick your flour weight, multiply each percentage to get the other ingredients' amounts.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Gluten-free dough is usually too wet** — open the lid during initial mix and scrape the sides with a rubber spatula if flour builds up.
- **Add a binder for better structure:** 1 tsp xanthan gum or 1 tbsp psyllium husk per cup of GF flour.
- **For richer flavor:** swap water for unsweetened plant milk (room temp; don't use delay timer).
- **Egg as binder:** add 1 egg (whisked into the water) — increases structure significantly. Reduce water by 50 ml to compensate.
- Expect a denser loaf than wheat bread — this is normal.
