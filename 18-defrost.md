# 18. Defrost

**Program:** 18 — Defrost
**Loaf size:** Not selectable
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Utility — gently warms frozen food

---

## About this program

Defrosts frozen food before cooking.

- **Default time:** 30 minutes
- **Adjustable range:** 0:10 to 2:00
- **Adjustment increment:** 10 minutes per press

---

## How to use

1. Place the frozen food into the bread pan (no kneading paddle needed — or leave it in, doesn't matter).
2. Lock pan in machine, close lid.
3. Press MENU until Program 18 (Defrost) appears.
4. Adjust time with + / − buttons (10-minute increments).
5. Press START.

---

## Use case notes

- **Bread / buns:** 10–15 minutes thaws a single loaf or roll.
- **Frozen dough:** 20–30 minutes brings it to room temperature for proofing.
- **Small portions of meat:** not ideal — this program is designed for room-temperature thawing, not safe partial-cooking. Use a microwave or fridge for meat.
- **Berries / fruit for jam:** 15–20 minutes is enough; follow with the Jam program.
