# 4. French Bread

**Program:** 4 — French Bread
**Loaf sizes available:** 500g, 750g, 1000g
**Crust color adjustable:** Yes (Light / Medium / Dark)

---

## About this program

A lean dough — high salt, low sugar, low fat. Produces a fluffy interior with a crispy crust. The longer fermentation builds flavor and chew.



---

## Ingredients — by loaf size

| Ingredient (add order) | 500g | 750g | 1000g | Manual notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water | 180 ml | 260 ml | 320 ml | Room temperature |
| **2.** Salt | 2 tsp (~12 g) | 2.5 tsp (~15 g) | 3 tsp (~18 g) | Put in corner — high for flavor |
| **3.** Sugar | 1.25 tbsp | 1.5 tbsp | 2 tbsp | Put in opposite corner |
| **4.** Oil | 1.25 tbsp | 1.5 tbsp | 2 tbsp | Low — keeps crust crisp |
| **5.** High-gluten (bread) flour | 2.25 cups / 300 g | 2.75 cups / 400 g | 3.5 cups / 500 g | — |
| **6.** Instant yeast | 1 tsp | 1.25 tsp | 1.5 tsp | On dry flour, no liquid contact |

> **Adding order:** Liquids first → salt & sugar in corners → oil → flour → **yeast last, on top of dry flour (must not touch liquid or salt).**

---

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

Flour is always 100%. Use this to scale to any flour weight.

| Ingredient | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | 100% | base |
| Water | 60–65% | hydration |
| Salt | 3–4% | **high** — defines the lean French style |
| Sugar | 4–5% | minimal |
| Oil | 3–5% | minimal — preserves crispy crust |
| Instant yeast | 0.9–1.5% | leavening |

**To scale:** pick your flour weight, multiply each percentage to get the other ingredients' amounts.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Not suitable for recipes with butter, margarine, or milk** (per manual). The lean dough is the point.
- **For more crust:** select Dark.
- **For Italian-style:** add ½ tsp dried herbs (rosemary, oregano) with the flour.
- **For seeded crust:** brush top with water before final rise and add sesame/poppy by hand if you can catch the timing — otherwise skip; this machine can't slash or top-seed automatically.
- The high salt is intentional — don't reduce it below 2.5% or you'll lose the French character.
