# 19. Stir-Fry

**Program:** 19 — Stir-Fry
**Loaf size:** Not selectable
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Dry-roasts ingredients by combining heat with the kneading paddle's stirring

---

## About this program

Kneads and bakes **dry ingredients like peanuts, soybeans, or other nuts/seeds** — effectively a dry-roasting program. The paddle keeps things moving so they roast evenly.

- **Default time:** 30 minutes (0:30)
- **Adjustable range:** 0:10 to 2:00
- **Adjustment increment:** ±1 minute per short press of + / −, ±10 minutes per long press

---

## Reference recipe — Roasted Peanuts

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Raw peanuts (with or without skin) | 300 g |

That's it — no oil needed (the paddle and heat dry-roast them).

---

## Method

1. Place raw nuts/seeds in the bread pan with the kneading paddle installed.
2. Lock in machine, close lid.
3. Select Program 19 (Stir-Fry).
4. Adjust time as needed (30 minutes is right for peanuts; smaller seeds may need less).
5. Press START.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Soybeans:** 30–40 minutes (they're harder than peanuts).
- **Cashews / almonds:** 15–20 minutes; check for golden color.
- **Sesame seeds:** 8–10 minutes only — they burn fast.
- **Pumpkin / sunflower seeds:** 15–20 minutes.
- **For salted/spiced nuts:** finish on the stovetop — toss roasted nuts with a little oil + salt + spices after the program ends. Adding oil during the program risks smoking.
- **Cool completely** before storing — warm nuts in a sealed container will go soft.
- **Listen for popping** — for seeds especially, popping means they're done or nearly done. Stop the program early if needed.
