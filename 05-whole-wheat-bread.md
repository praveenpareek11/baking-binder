# 5. Whole-Wheat Bread

**Program:** 5 — Whole-Wheat Bread
**Loaf sizes available:** 500g, 750g, 1000g
**Crust color adjustable:** Yes (Light / Medium / Dark)

---

## About this program

A blended dough — equal parts bread flour and whole-wheat flour. The program has a longer preheat to let whole grains hydrate. Produces a crispy thick crust.



---

| Ingredient (add order) | 500g | 750g | 1000g | Manual notes |

| **4.** Oil | 2 tbsp | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | — |

> **Adding order:** Liquids first → salt & sugar in corners → oil → flour → **yeast last, on top of dry flour (must not touch liquid or salt).**

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

Flour is always 100%. Use this to scale to any flour weight.

| Ingredient | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | 50% | structure |
| Whole-wheat flour | 50% | flavor + nutrition |
| **Total flour** | **100%** | — |
| Water | 60–65% | hydration |
| Salt | 0.6–1.5% | flavor + yeast control |
| Sugar | 9–11% | yeast food + softer crumb |
| Oil | 7–9% | softness |
| Instant yeast | 1.3–1.8% | slightly higher than Basic (whole grain is heavier) |

**To scale:** pick your flour weight, multiply each percentage to get the other ingredients' amounts.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Do not use the delay timer** (per manual) — whole grains absorbing water for hours produces poor results.
- **For 100% whole-wheat:** swap all bread flour for whole-wheat. Expect a denser, shorter loaf. Increase yeast by ¼ tsp.
- **For a softer crumb:** add 1–2 tbsp vital wheat gluten with the flours.
- **For multigrain:** replace some whole-wheat with oats, rye, or seeds (max ~20% of total flour weight).
- Whole-wheat loaves are naturally shorter and denser than white — this is normal, not a failure.
