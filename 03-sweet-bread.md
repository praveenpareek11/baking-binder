# 3. Sweet Bread

**Program:** 3 — Sweet Bread
**Loaf sizes available:** 500g, 750g, 1000g
**Crust color adjustable:** Yes (Light / Medium / Dark)

---

## About this program

For breads enriched with fruit juices, grated coconut, raisins, dried fruits, chocolate, or extra sugar. Has a longer rising phase to produce a light, airy texture. Lower salt than Basic.



---

## Ingredients — by loaf size

| Ingredient (add order) | 500g | 750g | 1000g | Manual notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water | 160 ml | 240 ml | 300 ml | Room temperature |
| **2.** Salt | 0.5 tsp (~3 g) | 0.5 tsp (~3 g) | 1 tsp (~6 g) | Put in corner |
| **3.** Sugar | 0.2 cup (~40 g) | 0.3 cup (~60 g) | 0.4 cup (~80 g) | Put in opposite corner |
| **4.** Oil | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | 4 tbsp | — |
| **5.** Milk powder | 1 tbsp | 1.5 tbsp | 2 tbsp | Adds richness |
| **6.** High-gluten (bread) flour | 2.25 cups / 300 g | 2.75 cups / 400 g | 3.5 cups / 500 g | — |
| **7.** Instant yeast | 1 tsp | 1.25 tsp | 1.5 tsp | On dry flour, no liquid contact |

> **Adding order:** Liquids first → salt & sugar in corners → oil → flour → **yeast last, on top of dry flour (must not touch liquid or salt).**

---

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

Flour is always 100%. Use this to scale to any flour weight.

| Ingredient | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | 100% | base |
| Water | 53–60% | lower than Basic (sugar adds moisture) |
| Salt | 0.6–1.2% | lower (more sugar already) |
| Sugar | 13–16% | substantially higher — defines the recipe |
| Oil | 8–11% | softness |
| Milk powder | 2.5–3% | richness |
| Instant yeast | 0.9–1.5% | leavening |

**To scale:** pick your flour weight, multiply each percentage to get the other ingredients' amounts.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Add-ins** (raisins, chocolate chips, candied fruit) go in at the beep cue. ½ cup per 750g loaf works well.
- **For fruit juice bread:** replace some/all of the water with juice (room temp; don't use delay timer if using juice with pulp).
- **For coconut bread:** add ¼–⅓ cup grated coconut at the beep cue.
- **Watch the crust:** high sugar darkens fast — start with Light or Medium crust setting.
- **If loaf rises too high:** reduce yeast by ¼ tsp.
