# 9. Poori Atta

**Program:** 9 — Poori Atta
**Loaf size:** 1000g only
**Crust color adjustable:** No
**Type:** Dough preparation (no baking) — for making pooris (deep-fried Indian bread)

---

## About this program

This program **stirs to let flour and liquids mix thoroughly** — it kneads a stiff dough for pooris. There is no rise or bake — you remove the dough afterward and fry pooris on the stovetop separately.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient (add order) | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water | 330 ml | Room temperature |
| **2.** Salt | 1 tsp | Put in corner |
| **3.** Oil | 3 tbsp | Adds softness to the poori |
| **4.** High-gluten flour | 4 cups / 560 g | Manual specifies high-gluten; for authentic poori, atta (whole-wheat) is more traditional |

> **No yeast** — pooris use unleavened dough.

---

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

| Ingredient | Baker's % |
| --- | --- |
| Flour | 100% |
| Water | ~59% |
| Salt | ~1.1% |
| Oil | ~8% |

---

## Tweak notes

- **For authentic poori:** use whole-wheat **atta** (chakki atta) instead of high-gluten flour. The manual's "high gluten" recommendation is incorrect for traditional poori — atta gives the right texture.
- **For softer pooris:** add 1 tbsp curd/yogurt + reduce water by 1 tbsp.
- **For ajwain (carom seed) flavor:** add ¼ tsp ajwain with the flour.
- **After the program ends:** remove dough, rest covered for 15–20 minutes, divide into small balls, roll thin, and deep-fry in hot oil (180°C) until puffed.
