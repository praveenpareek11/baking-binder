# 2. Quick Bread

**Program:** 2 — Quick Bread
**Loaf sizes available:** 500g, 750g, 1000g
**Crust color adjustable:** Yes (Light / Medium / Dark)

---

## About this program

Faster than Basic Bread. Same ingredients as Basic but with more yeast and **warm water (40–50°C)** to speed fermentation. Bread interior is denser than Basic but lighter than Whole-Wheat.

> ⚠️ Water temperature **must** be 40–50°C. Cold water will fail this program.

---

## Ingredients — by loaf size

| Ingredient (add order) | 500g | 750g | 1000g | Manual notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water (40–50°C) | 180 ml | 260 ml | 320 ml | **Warm**, not room temp |
| **2.** Salt | 1 tsp | 1.5 tsp | 2 tsp | Put in corner |
| **3.** Sugar | 2 tbsp | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | Put in opposite corner |
| **4.** Oil | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | 4 tbsp | — |
| **5.** High-gluten (bread) flour | 2.25 cups / 300 g | 2.75 cups / 400 g | 3.5 cups / 500 g | — |
| **6.** Instant yeast | 2.5 tsp (~7.5 g) | 3 tsp (~9 g) | 3.5 tsp (~10.5 g) | On dry flour, no liquid contact |

> **Adding order:** Liquids first → salt & sugar in corners → oil → flour → **yeast last, on top of dry flour (must not touch liquid or salt).**

---

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

Flour is always 100%. Use this to scale to any flour weight.

| Ingredient | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | 100% | base |
| Water (warm) | 64–65% | hydration |
| Salt | 1.2–2% | flavor + yeast control |
| Sugar | 7–8% | yeast food |
| Oil | 8–11% | softness |
| Instant yeast | 2–2.5% | **higher than Basic** — drives the faster program |

**To scale:** pick your flour weight, multiply each percentage to get the other ingredients' amounts.

---

## Tweak notes

- The extra yeast + warm water is what makes this "Quick." Don't substitute cold water.
- Denser result than Basic Bread is normal — the dough has less time to develop.
- Same add-in rules as Basic Bread (cue at 10 beeps).
- **Do not use the delay timer** — warm water won't stay warm during a delay.
