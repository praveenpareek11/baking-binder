# 6. Rice Bread

**Program:** 6 — Rice Bread
**Loaf sizes available:** 500g, 750g, 1000g
**Crust color adjustable:** Yes (Light / Medium / Dark)

---

## About this program

Bread made with cooked rice mixed into the dough — gives a soft, moist crumb. Use **cooled cooked rice** (not raw or hot).



---

## Ingredients — by loaf size

| Ingredient (add order) | 500g | 750g | 1000g | Manual notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.** Water | 100 ml | 160 ml | 200 ml | Room temperature |
| **2.** Salt | 1 tsp | 1.5 tsp | 2 tsp | Put in corner |
| **3.** Sugar | 2 tbsp | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | Put in opposite corner |
| **4.** Oil | 2.5 tbsp | 3 tbsp | 4 tbsp | — |
| **5.** Cooked rice (cooled) | 1 cup | 1.5 cups | 2 cups | **Cooled** — not hot |
| **6.** High-gluten (bread) flour | 1 cup / 140 g | 1.5 cups / 210 g | 2 cups / 280 g | — |
| **7.** Instant yeast | 1.25 tsp | 1.5 tsp | 2 tsp | On dry flour, no liquid contact |

> **Adding order:** Liquids first → salt & sugar in corners → oil → flour → **yeast last, on top of dry flour (must not touch liquid or salt).**

---

## Baker's percentages (for scaling)

Flour is always 100%. Use this to scale to any flour weight.

| Ingredient | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | 100% | base |
| Cooked rice (by weight) | ~100% | ~1:1 ratio with flour (the program's defining feature) |
| Water | 50–70% | lower because rice carries moisture |
| Salt | 4–7% | (higher % because flour weight is lower than other breads) |
| Sugar | 12–17% | yeast food |
| Oil | 14–25% | softness |
| Instant yeast | 2–3% | higher — heavier dough |

**To scale:** pick your flour weight, multiply each percentage to get the other ingredients' amounts.

---

## Tweak notes

- **Use cooled, cooked white rice.** Hot rice kills yeast; sticky/glutinous rice will overproof.
- **Texture is moister and softer than Basic.** Great for sandwich bread.
- **Don't reduce water further** — rice absorbs liquid during baking.
- **For brown rice:** works fine; expect a nuttier flavor and slightly denser crumb.
- This recipe's baker's % math is off-pattern because rice contributes both starch and water — the % above is for reference only; follow the table for best results.
